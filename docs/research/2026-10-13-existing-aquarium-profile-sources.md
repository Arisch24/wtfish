Manufacturer and trade-association guidance is cited for factual recommendations, without copying text or asserting a blanket content licence. Wikipedia factual cross-checks use pages marked CC BY-SA 4.0; descriptions are newly written. Seriously Fish was excluded after reading its September 2026 commercial reuse policy, which requires written permission even for restated facts. FishBase and the IUCN site/API were not used as content sources.

Tank volumes supplied in US gallons were converted using 3.785411784 litres per gallon and rounded upward to whole litres. Fahrenheit ranges were converted to Celsius and rounded to one decimal. Published metric figures were kept as published. Aquarium recommendations describe captivity, not tolerance experiments or wild temperature extremes.

## Taxonomic checks

- Catalog of Fishes cached genus entries confirm Paracheirodon innesi, Paracheirodon axelrodi, Pterophyllum scalare and Synchiropus splendidus.
- Catalog record spid=4810 explicitly gives Osteogaster aenea as the current valid name. The bronze profile now uses it and retains Corydoras aeneus as a searchable alias.
- Direct Catalog pages for several other genera returned 403 or unavailable. Their existing scientific names remain unchanged; a successful GBIF import is a separate check and does not establish Catalog verification.

## Deliberate omissions and limits

- No Malaysian occurrence status was inferred from aquarium sales, ornamental breeding, or broad Indo-Pacific/Southeast Asian ranges. This pass did not establish Malaysia status for the guppy or goldfish.
- Freshwater angelfish maximum length is omitted because the sources used in this pass did not establish a species maximum.
- Published adult or maximum sizes differ between sources. Cardinal, guppy, bronze, zebra, oscar and blue tang size figures follow their cited supplier/manufacturer species sheets; they are not claimed as fishing-record measurements.
- Photos and imported taxonomy/IUCN fields were preserved for the coordinating importer and photo review.
- A dated review means the displayed facts were checked against the listed sources; it does not represent a new field survey.
